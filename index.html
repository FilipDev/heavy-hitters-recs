🎬 192 Movie Recommendations — Stremio Addon

Your 192-film recommendation list as a native Stremio catalog. Once installed, the films appear inside Stremio under All 192 Recommendations, List A — First Picks (18) and The Numbered List (174) — where Play, Chromecast and Add to Library are all built in. This addon only provides the catalog; streams come from your other installed addons.

Install in the Stremio app

What you get

  • All 192 films, in the original recommendation order, with posters, year, genres, runtime and the same “powerfully captures…” descriptions as the dashboard.
  • Every film opens Stremio's normal detail page — add it to your library, pick a stream from your addons, and cast to your Chromecast from the player.
  • No account, no tracking, no server code: this addon is a handful of static files.
